What is PIH?

Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation

PIH occurs when the skin produces excess melanin in response to inflammation or injury. Common causes include:

  • Acne: Popping or picking pimples leaves dark marks
  • Injuries: Cuts, burns, or friction on skin
  • Irritation: Harsh products, waxing, or chemical burns
  • Insect bites: Scratching causes inflammatory marks
Recommended Actives:
Niacinamide 5-10% Alpha Arbutin 2% Vitamin C 10-20% Azelaic Acid 10%

Sun Damage

UV-Induced Hyperpigmentation

UV radiation stimulates melanocytes to produce more melanin. Chronic sun exposure leads to:

  • Sunspots (Solar Lentigines): Flat brown spots on sun-exposed areas
  • Melasma: Larger patches, often on cheeks and forehead
  • Freckles darkening: Existing freckles become more prominent
  • Photoaging: Premature wrinkles + pigmentation combo
Prevention is Key:
SPF 50+ Daily Reapply Every 2hrs Tinted Sunscreen Antioxidants (Vit C)

Uneven Skin Tone

Common Causes
  • Hormonal changes (pregnancy, birth control)
  • Chronic sun exposure without SPF
  • Post-acne marks and scarring
  • Pollution and environmental stress
  • Skin friction from clothing/accessories
  • Genetic predisposition
Treatment Approach
  • Step 1: Sun protection (SPF 50+ daily)
  • Step 2: Vitamin C serum (AM)
  • Step 3: Alpha arbutin or niacinamide
  • Step 4: Gentle exfoliation (AHA 2x/week)
  • Step 5: Azelaic acid for stubborn spots
  • Step 6: Patience — 6-12 weeks minimum
Hero Ingredients
Vitamin C (L-Ascorbic Acid) Alpha Arbutin Niacinamide Azelaic Acid Kojic Acid Licorice Extract Tranexamic Acid Mandelic Acid

Prevention Strategies

DO
  • Apply SPF 50+ sunscreen every morning — even indoors
  • Reapply sunscreen every 2-3 hours when outdoors
  • Use tinted sunscreen with iron oxides (blocks visible light)
  • Apply Vitamin C serum every morning under sunscreen
  • Treat acne early to prevent PIH marks
  • Be patient — pigmentation treatment takes months
  • Wear protective clothing and hats
  • Use a consistent routine — don't skip days
DON'T
  • Pick, pop, or squeeze pimples — this causes PIH
  • Skip sunscreen — it undoes all brightening work
  • Use lemon juice on skin — too acidic, causes burns
  • Over-exfoliate — damaged barrier worsens pigmentation
  • Mix retinol with AHA in the same routine
  • Expect overnight results — be realistic
  • Use hydroquinone without medical supervision
  • Use harsh scrubs on hyperpigmented areas

Sample Pigmentation-Fighting Routine

Morning (AM)
  1. Gentle cleanser (no harsh actives)
  2. Vitamin C 15-20% serum — wait 3 min
  3. Niacinamide 5% moisturizer
  4. Tinted SPF 50+ sunscreen (iron oxides)
Night (PM)
  1. Gentle cleanser
  2. Alpha arbutin 2% serum — wait 2 min
  3. Azelaic acid 10% on dark spots — wait 5 min
  4. Moisturizer with ceramides
Add mandelic acid 5% peel 2x/week instead of step 2-3
